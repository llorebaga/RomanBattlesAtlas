01

EVENT OVERVIEW

Zama ended the Second Punic War, confined Carthage to Africa under harsh terms, and earned Scipio the name ‘Africanus’.

Scipio defeated Hannibal in a pitched battle in which Numidian cavalry, now largely on the Roman side, proved decisive; the elephant charge was blunted and the Carthaginian line eventually enveloped.

The battle brought the war’s two greatest commanders together on open ground. Its most consequential feature was the reversal of the cavalry balance: Masinissa’s Numidians now fought for Rome, undoing the advantage that had won Cannae.

EVIDENCE REGISTER

What remains uncertain

  • The battlefield has never been securely located.
  • Whether the site was Zama, Naraggara, or elsewhere is debated.
  • Army sizes and casualty figures are literary.

This page separates surviving testimony from modern reconstruction. It does not invent precise tracks, dialogue, weather, or unit positions.
