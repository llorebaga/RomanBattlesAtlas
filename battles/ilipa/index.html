EVENT OVERVIEW

Ilipa secured the peninsula for Rome and freed Scipio to plan the invasion of Africa.

Scipio reversed his usual order of battle to set his best troops on the wings and enveloped the Carthaginian army, effectively ending Carthaginian power in Iberia.
